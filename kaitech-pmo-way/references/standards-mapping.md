# Pemetaan ke Standar

| Praktik Kaitech | PMBOK 7 | PRINCE2 7 | ISO 21502 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gate G0–G9 dengan approver | Development Approach & Life Cycle domain | Manage by stages; stage boundary | Project life cycle |
| Charter & sponsor | Stakeholders domain; principle Stewardship | Business case; Directing a project | Initiating |
| Baseline terkunci + CR | Planning & Delivery domains | Change practice; baselines | Planning; Change control |
| Progress berbasis bukti | Measurement domain | Progress (product-based) | Progress control |
| Toleransi & eksepsi | Measurement; tailoring | Manage by exception; tolerances | Progress control |
| RAID | Uncertainty domain | Risk & Issues | Risk; Issue management |
| Health score & RAG | Measurement domain | Highlight report | Reporting |
| Lessons learned → skill | Team domain; principle Adaptability | Learn from experience | Lessons learned |
| Stakeholder & WA plan | Stakeholders domain | Communication management approach | Stakeholder engagement |

Kaitech memakai PRINCE2 untuk struktur kontrol (gate, toleransi, eksepsi) dan PMBOK 7 untuk
prinsip dan domain kinerja. Klien BUMN umumnya mengenal keduanya, dan auditor menilai jejak
keputusan, bukan metodologi tertentu.
